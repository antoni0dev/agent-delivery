description: Turn a focused discussion into a scoped Linear ticket or master initiative, preserving decisions and acceptance criteria. Use when the user asks to draft or create a ticket.
---

Read [project configuration](../../../.agent-harness/PROJECT.md) and the intake guidance in [WORKFLOW.md](../../../.agent-harness/WORKFLOW.md). Links target the installed layout; source templates are under root `templates/`.

Inspect the relevant repository and approved decision sources. Draft a short action title and plain-language outcome, constraints and observable acceptance criteria. Put architectural detail below the concise request. Preserve unresolved product decisions explicitly and avoid inventing behavior.

Use only this repository's configured tracker connection and scope. Create or update tickets when authorized, checking for duplicates and concurrent edits first. Preserve the original request. Ticket creation alone does not delegate execution; hand it to an initiative owner only when explicitly requested or covered by standing authority.
