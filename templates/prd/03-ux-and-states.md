## States

| Surface | Loading | Empty | Error | Success | Design node |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Cover disabled, permission-denied and partial-data states where they exist. For asynchronous or on-chain actions also cover pending, confirmed, failed, rejected by the user, and replaced or dropped. Loading never hides behind a zero, an empty list or "no selection".
