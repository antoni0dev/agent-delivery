# Dependencies and risks

## Dependencies

| Service, contract or third party | Direction | Purpose | Contract artifact | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| | We depend on it, or it depends on us | | OpenAPI document, stream contract, ABI or indexer schema | Available, in progress or missing |

A missing contract artifact is a dependency risk, not permission to infer the interface.
